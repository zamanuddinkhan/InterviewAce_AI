Terms & Conditions

These usage rules describe this interview practice project.

  • Use the platform for learning and interview preparation. AI feedback may be incomplete or incorrect; review it yourself. Scores are practice feedback and do not determine hiring outcomes.
  • Keep your login and recovery code private. Only upload materials you have permission to use, and avoid including sensitive personal information.
  • Respect other participants. Join rooms by invitation, obtain consent before recording, and do not attempt to access another user's private account or files.
  • Use coding features for the assigned exercise. Do not submit malicious code, attempt to bypass isolation or disrupt the platform.
  • Camera and microphone access is optional and controlled by your browser. Recorded-answer processing requires explicit consent.
  • Availability depends on the host PC, database, local AI models and network. Sessions may be interrupted by service restarts or connectivity issues.
  • You can update your profile and delete your account from Profile & security. Contact the maintainers for installation or support questions.

Privacy Policy

Account and practice data

The application stores your first and last names, contact number, email address, password hash, session records, uploaded files and interview activity. Contact details stay private to your account. Room files, conversation and reports are restricted to room participants; question drafts remain private to the interviewer.

Camera, microphone and AI

Devices are accessed only after you enable them. A camera preview does not automatically create a recording. Peer calls send media to the other participant through WebRTC. Consented audio transcription uses a local model; temporary audio files are deleted after processing. Solo transcripts are returned as drafts and become saved answers only when you submit them. Derived peer speech metrics may be shared within the room.

Questions, answers and relevant PDF excerpts are processed by the configured local AI services. This project does not require a paid external AI API.

Cookies, hosting and preferences

An HttpOnly session cookie keeps you signed in. Your browser stores the light/dark preference locally. When this app is deployed with a Vercel frontend and a PC backend, browser API traffic, including the session cookie and submitted content, passes through Vercel's HTTPS proxy before reaching the PC. Tailscale Funnel carries the backend connection to the PC. The PC stores the MySQL database, uploads and local AI models. Review Vercel's Privacy Notice and Tailscale's Privacy Policy for their handling of hosting and network traffic. The project does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Manage your data

You can edit your profile, remove supported uploads or interview records, log out all devices and delete your account from the workspace. Retained backups follow the host operator's retention policy and are separate from the live database. Ask the maintainers about retained backup copies or this installation's operation.
