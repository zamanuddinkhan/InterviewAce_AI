DBMS Interview Study Notes
A primary key uniquely identifies a row. A foreign key references another table's key and helps maintain referential integrity.
Normalization reduces duplication and update anomalies. In first normal form values are atomic; second normal form removes partial dependencies; third normal form removes transitive dependencies from non-key attributes.
An index can speed up certain database queries but adds storage and can slow down writes. Evaluate with realistic query patterns.
A database transaction groups operations. ACID stands for Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, and Durability.
SQL joins combine records across tables. An INNER JOIN returns matching records, while a LEFT JOIN also keeps unmatched left-side rows.
