HR Mock Interview Study Notes
Behavioral answers can use the STAR approach: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Explain your own contribution and a specific measurable outcome when available.
For 'Tell me about yourself', give a short summary: current background, relevant abilities, example project, and target role. Avoid unrelated private information.
When explaining teamwork conflict, describe the situation respectfully, your communication, and what you learned. Do not blame other people unnecessarily.
For strengths and weaknesses, include credible examples and improvement actions. An interviewer may ask for a specific experience as a follow-up.
